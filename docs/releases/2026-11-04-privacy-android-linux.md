# Privacy policy for Android and Linux, 2026-11-04

`privacy.html` now also covers RoRadio 2.4.0 for Android on Google Play and for Linux in the Snap Store and as a
package: Google Play requires the policy it links to to apply to the app it lists. What is collected does not change:
the same diagnostics, off the same switch in Settings. The diagnostics row names the operating system in general, cloud
sync is marked as Windows and Xbox only, and captions run "on your device" instead of "on your PC".
