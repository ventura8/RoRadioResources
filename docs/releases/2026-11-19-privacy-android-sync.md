# Privacy policy: Android / Google cloud sync and on-device captions, 2026-11-19

`privacy.html` matches RoRadio 2.5.0 on Google Play:

- Cloud sync is no longer "Windows and Xbox only": Microsoft or Google sign-in on Windows, Xbox, Android and Linux,
  including Android's in-app Google picker and the QR code flow.
- Live captions explicitly do not use the microphone; they decode the station stream on the device.

Play Console Data safety and the paste text in `packaging/android/play/listing-en-US.md` must stay aligned with this
page.
