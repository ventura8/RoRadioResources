# Device E2E on a TV, 2026-11-14

`scripts/e2e/Test-ReceiverOnTablet.ps1` now also runs on a Cast device without a touch screen, such as the Google
TV Streamer. No change to the receiver page.

## What changed

- **No touch screen.** The script finds whether the device has a touch screen in `dumpsys input`, or takes
  `-NoTouch`. Without one, next, previous, next again and pause are the presses a remote or a sender makes: queue
  jumps and a PAUSE over Cast, through `cast-client.mjs jump`. Each must still give exactly one step. A touch
  display still taps its system media bar.
- **The layout check on a TV.** On the TV it threw "Cannot index into a null array". It read the boxes from the
  touch tap's answer, which a TV never has. The check now reads the page's `shown` status itself. It scales the
  boxes by the screenshot (a 1920 x 1080 TV draws a 960 x 540 viewport at scale 2), and reserves the system bar's
  area only on a touch display. A missing report is a failure with its reason, not an exception.
- **`cast-client.mjs avail`** asks the device whether it can run the RoRadio receiver (`GET_APP_AVAILABILITY`). A
  stale cached app config answers `APP_UNAVAILABLE`.
- The script's messages say "device", not "tablet".

## Checked

- On the Google TV Streamer (192.168.1.107), every step passed: launch, load, song, theme on and off, layout on
  the screenshot, next, previous, next again (one step each), pause, play, switch and stop. `avail` answered
  `APP_AVAILABLE`.
