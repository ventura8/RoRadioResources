# Live captions: Canary's DirectML graph and the privacy policy, 2026-10-11

RoRadio 2.1.0 adds live captions (experimental, off by default): a speech model on the listener's PC turns what the
station says into text. Two things it needs live here.

## `captions/canary-1b-v2-encoder-model.dml.onnx`

The graph of NVIDIA Canary-1B-v2's encoder (CC BY 4.0; ONNX export by istupakov, commit
`5ebc1520cef7b6b318b3526ad17adbfe00bc1bfc`) that DirectML can run. DirectML 1.24 (Windows ML's ONNX Runtime) throws
on the export's Reshape nodes that carry `allowzero=1` (225 of 231, `node_view_189` first, `0x8007023E`), so the
accurate model was stuck on the CPU. None of those shapes holds a literal 0, so `allowzero=0` means the same thing.

- Made by `scripts/captions/canary_dml_graph.py` from the export's `encoder-model.onnx`; only that attribute changes.
- 4,762,393 bytes, SHA-256 `52fa8e6bd54d79435e3c4d2d1e2c7367fd9c3a18523fd081274d2fffa1743363`. RoRadio downloads
  it pinned to this repository's commit and checks the hash.
- It holds no weights: it points at the export's unchanged `encoder-model.onnx.data`, which the app downloads from
  Hugging Face next to it.
- Measured on an RTX 5090: 0.049 s for 7 s of audio (the CPU takes 0.6 s), within 3e-5 of the CPU's fp32 output.

## `privacy.html`

A "Live captions" section:

- audio and recognised text stay on the device and are not stored;
- captions open a second connection to the station;
- the model is downloaded after the listener agrees, from Hugging Face, GitHub and this site, which see the IP
  address and the file;
- Settings › Downloaded caption models › Delete removes it;
- diagnostics may count caption use (model, graphics card or not, timing), never what was said.

The date at the top moves to 11 October 2026.
