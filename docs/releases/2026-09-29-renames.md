# Stations under their current names, 2026-09-29

Twelve stations are listed under the name their stream carries today, and one station that duplicated another
is removed (owner's decision, 2026-09-29). Each GUID stays, so listeners' favorites keep working and show the new
name; every RoRadio app downloads the list from
[GitHub Pages](https://ventura8.github.io/RoRadioResources/RadioStationsData.json), so the names change without an
app update.

The evidence is what the 2026-09-29 revival recorded for each stream: its own `icy-name` and `icy-url`, and the
broadcaster's site. Urls, categories and logos do not change here; the logos of the new brands wait in
ventura8/RoRadioResources#3.
